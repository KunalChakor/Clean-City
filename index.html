🧠 How Smart Routing Works

This real-time data does more than just show you fill levels. It powers our city's smart waste collection system:

  • 1. Data Collection: Each smart bin has a sensor that measures its fill level and sends the data to our central system every hour.
  • 2. Route Optimization: Our system analyzes which bins are full or nearly full (typically >85%). It then calculates the most efficient route for the collection truck to take, skipping bins that are mostly empty.
  • 3. Dispatch: The optimized route is sent directly to the driver's navigation device, saving fuel, reducing emissions, and preventing overflowing bins.
